About the Map

Data, history, and tools behind the Door County Shipwrecks Webmap

Introduction, Motivation & Objective

What are we mapping?

This map documents the 220 shipwrecks that have been recorded in Door County waters. Each wreck is plotted at it's respective location and includes information about the specific wreck; vessel name, type, year built, cause of wreck, length of ship, and lives lost.

Who is the intended audience?

This map is really intended for anyone with an interest in maratime hisotry, the Great Lakes, shipwrecks, and Door County. No prior knowledge is necessary while looking at this map.

Why does this map matter?

This map brings to life ships and shipwrecks that occured hundreds of years ago that are often overlooked and forgot about. This map tells it's users a story with the different layers that show patterns of the wrecks and perhaps why these wrecks occured, as well as piecing together any coorelation.

Datasets

This map utilizes datasets from various sources. The layer, Shipwrecks (Great lakes), Wisconsin 2014, includes the locations and information about the respective shipwrecks. This layer was utilized from the State Cartographers Office. The two toggleable basemaps, (topo and bathymetric) are both from ESRI.

Methodology

The first step that was taken was in ArcGIS. The shipwreck data was reprojected to WGS84, from their it was cleaned up and clipped to just the Door County boundary. Unneeded data from the attribute table was removed ensuring less confusion in the future.

Data Delivery

The shipwreck data was delivered to the coding program, Phcode, via a GeoJSON file which was created in ArcGIS.

Project Summary

A few things that work very well as well as some strengths in this webmap are the toggleable layers and the legends that are very clearcut. It would be very hard to misinterpret what this webmap is delivering. The symbology is very appealing too, it creates for an enjoyable experience. The design also improves the enjoyable experience that users will toggle with. Everything is very symmetric as well as organized. This map also has a couple of limitations. There is some data gaps within the popups. For example, where the ship was built, length, and year are sometimes null or blank. There really isn't anything that can be done about this. The bathymetric layer also has a maximum zoom before it starts throwing out 'No Data' messages. I would like to improve on some data discrepencies as well as finding a different bathymetric
